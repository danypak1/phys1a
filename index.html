What a question looks like

A vector has Aₓ = −5.0 m and Aᵧ = +5.0 m. Your calculator returns arctan(Aᵧ/Aₓ) = −45°. Measured counterclockwise from the +x-axis, the direction is

A−45°
B135°
C315°
D225°
✗ Not quite — the answer is (B) What this mistake means: you added 180° to the calculator's −45° without checking the signs of the components. Aₓ < 0 with Aᵧ > 0 puts the vector in the second quadrant, up and to the left — 225° points down and to the left.
